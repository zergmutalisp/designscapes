# Asset credits

## Opening portrait

Jacques-Louis David, _The Emperor Napoleon in His Study at the Tuileries_, 1812, oil on canvas. National Gallery of Art, Washington, Samuel H. Kress Collection, accession 1961.9.15.

- Collection and public-domain status: https://www.nga.gov/artworks/46114-emperor-napoleon-his-study-tuileries
- Local image: `public/assets/napoleon-david.jpg`
- Downloaded at 1200px width from the NGA's IIIF service; no generative alteration. CSS applies cropping, desaturation and dark overlays. The page explains the painting's constructed political message.

## Cartographic geography

Natural Earth physical land, 1:110 million, distributed by `world-atlas` as TopoJSON. Public domain. https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas

No contemporary political borders are displayed. All route paths and labels are rendered locally in SVG. The Saint Helena silhouette is an original symbolic illustration explicitly labeled as such.

## Typography

Cormorant Garamond and Inter, distributed locally via Fontsource. Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License. License text is included in `public/assets/CORMORANT-LICENSE.txt` and `public/assets/INTER-LICENSE.txt` and is copied into production builds. The browser downloads bundled local font files.

No remote runtime images or fonts are required.
